# ATLAS design direction

Sumber: brief pemilik proyek. Agen hanya merapikan, tidak menambah klaim merek.

## Product
- Name: ATLAS
- Type: general-purpose e-commerce marketplace
- Tagline: Everything You Need. One Destination.
- Categories in scope: electronics, fashion, home, beauty, sports, accessories, everyday essentials

## Positioning
- Premium but accessible
- Modern and technology-driven
- Trustworthy, practical, scalable
- Suitable for many categories and sellers later

## Personality
- Confident
- Minimalist
- Intelligent
- Global
- Modern rather than overly futuristic

## Visual language
- Premium editorial commerce
- Minimalist, bold typography
- Carefully composed product photography
- Generous whitespace
- Strong visual hierarchy
- Refined grid layouts
- Consistent spacing and alignment
- Subtle transitions and purposeful interactions
- Clear navigation and readable product information

## Palette (starting point)
- Primary background: warm white or very light neutral
- Main text: near-black
- Secondary text: neutral gray
- Accent: restrained electric blue or cobalt blue
- Optional dark surfaces for a later dark theme
- Borders: subtle neutrals
- Core colors stay within 2 to 3 plus one accent

## Typography
- Modern, highly readable sans-serif
- Clear hierarchy for headings, product titles, prices, labels, descriptions
- Avoid excessive uppercase
- Avoid oversized type that harms usability

## Content rules
- No fake sales statistics or invented customer counts
- No fake testimonials
- Promotional banners only when configured
- Product images come from real stored URLs, with fallbacks
- Logo: wordmark text "ATLAS" until the owner supplies a mark

## Dials
- ENERGY 2 (assured editorial, not loud agency work)
- RHYTHM 2 (consistent system with a few section breaks)
- MOTION 1 (hover and small transitions only; respect reduced motion)

## Out of scope for visuals
- Excessive gradients, glassmorphism, floating shapes
- Repetitive identical-weight card grids as the whole page
- Oversized empty heroes
- Emoji standing in for product photos
- Default starter-template dashboards
